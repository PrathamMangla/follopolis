# Follopolis: channel setup

**Name:** Follopolis ("follow" + "polis", the Greek word for city)
**Meaning:** the city built by its followers.

**Done:** Instagram @follopolis (photo, name and bio set), website live. **Still to do:** the website link in the Instagram bio (phone app only) and the YouTube channel.

---

## Step 1: Images (2 minutes)

1. Run the local server (see README) and open http://localhost:8080/brand.html
2. Wait about 10 seconds. It takes pictures of your city.
3. Download the **banner** (2560×1440, for YouTube) and the **profile picture** (800×800, for YouTube and Instagram).

Run it again whenever the city has grown, so the images always show your real city.

---

## Step 4: YouTube

Go to youtube.com → your profile picture → **Create a channel**.

- **Name:** `Follopolis`
- **Handle:** `@follopolis`
- **Profile picture and banner:** from Step 1.
- **Description:**
  ```
  Follopolis is a city built by its followers.

  🏠 Follow us on Instagram and you get a house in the city.
  💬 Comment a job and your citizen starts working: baker, pirate, wizard, dragon tamer…
  🏪 Got a business? Add it and your house gets a shop sign.

  Every day the city grows. Find your house: https://prathammangla.github.io/follopolis
  ```
- **Links** (Customization → Basic info): Website → `https://prathammangla.github.io/follopolis`, Instagram → `https://instagram.com/follopolis`
- **Keywords** (Settings → Channel): `city builder, built by followers, follopolis, lowpoly city, community game, day by day, timelapse`
- **Channel trailer:** your best reel so far.

---

## Step 5: What to post on YouTube

**Shorts (every day):** upload the same reel you post on Instagram. Title: `Day 12: 420 strangers built this city 🏘️ #shorts`

**Long videos (once you have 10k Instagram followers):**
| Title | What it is |
|---|---|
| I let 10,000 strangers build a city for 30 days | The whole story so far, day by day, with voiceover |
| The Great Dragon Incident of Day 40 | One big city event as a story |
| Every weird job my followers asked for | Funniest comments and what you built |
| These real businesses moved into my city | Shop owners, their signs, why they joined |

Shorts get people to find you. Long videos are what earn money on YouTube (the Partner Program needs 1,000 subscribers and 4,000 hours watched).
